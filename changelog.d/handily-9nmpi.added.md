- **filetree work view fits a large repo.** A chain of folders with one child folder at each
  level now shows as one row, such as `src/api/`. The work view shows at most 40 rows. A more
  row such as `[ +9 changed in 6 folders ]` counts the changed files that it hides and the
  folders that hold them, and a press on it shows them all. The tree stops at 1,000 rows and
  says so: `1,000 rows drawn, the most a pane takes.` with `Close a folder, or [ work only ].`
  While an agent is picked or in view, the folder rows keep their git counts (handily-9nmpi).
