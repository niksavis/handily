- **reply-view: a point with several entries draws as a bar that holds the point.** The
  flowchart layout spread the entries of a point over a wider box, but it drew one `◉` at the
  left edge of that box. So some arrows ended in empty space. Now each arrow ends on a `━` bar
  (a `┃` bar in a diagram that draws across), and the point stands on the bar at the middle. An
  edge that leaves the point starts at the point. A point with one entry or no entry, such as
  `[*]` in a state diagram, draws as before. (handily-gb5l08)
