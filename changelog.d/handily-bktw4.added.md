- **reply-view lists your prompts in a prompts tab and jumps between them.** `/replies prompts`
  opens a `prompts` tab with one row for each prompt that you typed: its number, its time, its
  first line and its count of tool calls. The selected prompt shows `▸`, the whole prompt in up to
  3 lines and the tools by name. A press on a row, the buttons `prev`, `next` and `last`, and the
  commands `/replies next`, `/replies prev` and `/replies prompt 9` move the transcript to that
  prompt in fullscreen. A jump that Claude Code refuses shows `could not jump to prompt` and the
  reason in words. A compaction draws a dim rule in the list (handily-bktw4).
