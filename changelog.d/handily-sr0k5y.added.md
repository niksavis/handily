- **reply-view: a reply appears once, in its final layout.** While the text of a reply streams on
  the terminal, the reply view holds it back, and the spinner line shows its progress, such as
  `writing reply · 12 lines · 1 table`. Text that streams longer than 8 seconds shows at once and
  streams on. Esc shows the held text at once, and the transcript keeps it. The `streaming`
  setting `live` streams replies as Claude Code draws them. (handily-sr0k5y)
