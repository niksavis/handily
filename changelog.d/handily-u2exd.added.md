- **The Session details show a context trend, the turns left before auto-compact and the context
  parts.** The trend has one bar for each main turn and the mean growth of the last 5 turns. A
  compaction starts it again. The auto-compact line names the threshold and the turns left, and
  says `soon` in the warning colour from 2 turns left. `[ context parts ]` lists the parts of the
  context with their tokens and their percent of the compaction window. `/board stats` prints the
  same lines as text. A host that gives no parts shows `no breakdown on this host`
  (handily-u2exd).
