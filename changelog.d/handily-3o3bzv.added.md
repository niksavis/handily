- **`/replies message` opens the message tab from the keyboard.** The `more` button of a message
  from another session takes a mouse click. `/replies message` opens the last such message in the
  `message` tab, and `/replies message 2` the one before it. The mod keeps the last 20 messages
  that the transcript drew in the session. A number out of range or a word gets a reply that names
  the range and the command. (handily-3o3bzv)
